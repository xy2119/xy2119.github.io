About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computing at Imperial College London, co-supervised by Prof. Abbas Edalat and Prof. Jiankang Deng.

My research is driven by the intersection of Computer Vision and 3D Graphics, specifically focused on 2D & 3D Face Generation, 3D face reconstruction, talking head generation, and digital humans. I work on generative models (including diffusion models and 3D Morphable Models) for realistic face synthesis, reenactment, and fine-grained facial dynamics control.

Prior to my PhD, I received my MEng in Materials Science and Engineering from Imperial College London (2019–2023), transitioning from biomedical data science (graph neural networks, knowledge graphs) and protein design with geometric deep learning at the Data Science Institute.

Publications

MoCoTalk: Multi-Conditional Diffusion with Adaptive Router for Controllable Talking Head Generation

Xinyan Ye, Jiankang Deng, Abbas Edalat

IJCB 2026 IEEE International Joint Conference on Biometrics (IJCB 2026)  ·  Rome, Italy

A multi-conditional video diffusion framework that unifies four complementary control signals — reference image, facial keypoints, 3DMM-rendered shading meshes, and speech audio — with an Adaptive Multi-Condition Router for fine-grained, controllable talking-head synthesis.

arXiv PDF

Embodied Empathy: A Multimodal AR and LLM-Powered System for Self-Attachment Psychotherapy with Self-Initiated Humour

Xinyan Ye, Gwyneth Phang, Anandha Gopalan, Abbas Edalat

ICMI 2026 Companion Publication of the 28th International Conference on Multimodal Interaction  ·  Napoli, Italy

A novel multimodal application operationalising the Self-Initiated Humour Protocol within the Self-Attachment Technique framework. It integrates customisable 3D childhood avatars, augmented reality, and an LLM-driven virtual therapist for automated emotion mirroring in digital mental health tools.

ACM arXiv

Uplift Modeling based on Graph Neural Network Combined with Causal Knowledge

Haowen Wang, Xinyan Ye, Yikang Wang, Yangze Zhou, Zhiyi Zhang, Longhan Zhang, Jing Jiang, Yiteng Zhai

IEEE CAI 2024 2024 IEEE Conference on Artificial Intelligence, pp. 1487-1492

A scalable uplift modeling framework combining Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) with causal knowledge via Conditional Average Treatment Effect (CATE) and adjacency structure learning to effectively estimate treatment effects.

Multihead Causal Distilling Weighting Is All You Need for Uplift Modeling

Haowen Wang, Xinyan Ye, Zhiyi Zhang, Yikang Wang

HPBD&IS 2022 2022 International Conference on High Performance Big Data and Intelligent Systems (HDIS)

Introduces Multihead Causal Distilling Weighting (MCDW) to capture heterogeneous treatment effects and isolate confounding variables from observational data for uplift modeling.
